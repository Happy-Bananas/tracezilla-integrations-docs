# Shopify

Use BifrostConnect to apply custom PHP business rules between Shopify and
tracezilla.

## Start here

1. Prepare [tracezilla](../tracezilla-fundamentals.html): a development company
   and a personal access token.
2. Complete the [Shopify prerequisites](./setup.html): a development store and
   an installed app.
3. Install the integration [with Docker](../run-with-docker.html) or
   [without Docker](../manual-installation.html).
4. Check both connections with `./check-connection`. Nothing else works until
   this passes.
5. Continue with [Implement Custom Business Logic](./custom-business-logic.html),
   or select one of the Shopify commands in the navigation.
